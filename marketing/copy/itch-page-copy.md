## Long description
**Stop fighting aspect ratios. Feed CapsuleForge your screenshots; get every store asset back.**

CapsuleForge is a 100% client-side studio that turns raw gameplay screenshots into
polished, art-directed marketing assets. Nothing is uploaded, nothing leaves your
machine — open it and work.

**Exact itch.io dimensions as presets**
itch cover 630×500 · thumbnail 315×250 · OG/Twitter card 1200×630 · square avatar 512×512 · banner 960×540 · wide capsule 800×450

**6 art-directed templates** — Ember, Ocean, Violet, Gold, Mono, Toxic. One click, whole new mood.

**Real workflow features**
- Drag-and-drop screenshots as backgrounds, with a crop slider
- Seeded sample key-art generator — evaluate the tool with zero assets
- Title, tagline, dev credit, accent + text colors — saved locally and reused every session
- Platform badges (Windows / macOS / Linux / Web / Android)
- 9 text anchors, auto title sizing, glow, vignette, gradient overlay, scanlines
- Batch export: every size in one click

**Works everywhere**
Desktop browser, mobile browser, and a downloadable Android app. Try it right here
on this page — the demo runs fully in your browser.
