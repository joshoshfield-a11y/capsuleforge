## Long description
**Your screenshots in, every store asset out.**

CapsuleForge is a key-art studio that runs entirely in your browser. Drop in a
screenshot, set your title, pick a look — export your itch cover, thumbnail,
OG card, and the rest. Nothing uploads, nothing leaves your machine.

**Built for the itch.io page**
itch cover 630×500 · thumbnail 315×250 · OG/Twitter card 1200×630 · square avatar 512×512 · banner 960×540 · wide capsule 800×450

**Steam preset pack** (full version)
header 920×430 · small 462×174 · main 1232×706 · vertical 748×896 · library capsule 600×900 · hero 3840×1240 · logo 1280×720

**What you actually do with it**
- Drop screenshots in as backdrops, nudge the crop until it sits right
- No screenshots yet? Generate a print-style stand-in (screenprint, halftone, or grainfield) to design against
- Pull a palette straight from your art so the type matches the game
- Start from an art-direction preset — Arcade Cabinet, Zine Cut, VHS Rental, Screen Print, Midnight Matinee — then tweak everything
- Title, tagline, studio credit, 13 fonts, 9 text positions, grain, scanlines, hard shadows, underline rules
- Platform badges (Windows / macOS / Linux / Web / Android)
- Contact-sheet preview: see your design at every export size side by side before you commit
- Export one size, every size, or everything as a single .zip — PNG or JPG

**Your stuff stays yours**
Everything renders locally in the tab. Your screenshots never touch a server.
Your settings are remembered between sessions.

**Try it here**
The demo on this page is the real tool, running in your browser. The full
version adds batch export of every size (including the Steam pack) and the
contact-sheet preview.
