# Devlog 01 — CapsuleForge is live (and it made its own cover)

Every game page lives or dies on its key art, and every dev I know loses an
afternoon to aspect-ratio tables and "why is my cover blurry" mysteries.
CapsuleForge is my answer: a 100% client-side studio that eats your screenshots
and spits out every asset itch.io asks for — cover, thumbnail, OG card, banner —
at the exact pixel dimensions, with six art-directed templates on top.

The fun part: this page's cover, thumbnail, and screenshots were all generated
by CapsuleForge itself. Dogfooding level: maximum.

Try the in-browser demo on the project page, and if it saves you an afternoon,
grab the full version — batch export and brand persistence are included.
Also available as an Android app for iterating from your phone.

Let me know what templates or store formats you want next.
